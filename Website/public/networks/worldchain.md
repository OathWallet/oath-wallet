# World Chain wallet: send and receive ETH

Receive, send and manage supported World Chain assets in Oath. Match the selected account, network and asset before using its receiving details.

## Receive World Chain

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose World Chain in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Open Receive and choose World Chain. Check that the sending service uses the same network and token identifier.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send ETH

1. Open Send and select the asset on World Chain. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Review the destination, selected World Chain asset and estimated network fee in Oath. The native fee asset for this account is ETH.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect only to compatible dApps and review the requested accounts, network and signing action before approving.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, World Chain network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive World Chain in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose World Chain in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Open Receive and choose World Chain. Check that the sending service uses the same network and token identifier.

### What pays the World Chain transaction fee?

ETH is the native fee asset for this network account. Review the destination, selected World Chain asset and estimated network fee in Oath. The native fee asset for this account is ETH.

### Can I connect World Chain dApps?

Connect only to compatible dApps and review the requested accounts, network and signing action before approving.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
