# Elysium wallet: send and receive HYPE

Receive, send and manage supported Elysium assets in Oath. Match the selected account, network and asset before using its receiving details.

## Receive Elysium

1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Elysium in Receive.
2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
3. Open Receive and choose Elysium. Check that the sending service uses the same network and token identifier.
4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

## Send HYPE

1. Open Send and select the asset on Elysium. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Review the destination, selected Elysium asset and estimated network fee in Oath. The native fee asset for this account is HYPE.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Connect only to compatible dApps and review the requested accounts, network and signing action before approving.

1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
3. Review the website, selected wallet, Elysium network and requested permissions before approving.
4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

## Questions

### How do I receive Elysium in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Elysium in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Open Receive and choose Elysium. Check that the sending service uses the same network and token identifier.

### What pays the Elysium transaction fee?

HYPE is the native fee asset for this network account. Review the destination, selected Elysium asset and estimated network fee in Oath. The native fee asset for this account is HYPE.

### Can I connect Elysium dApps?

Connect only to compatible dApps and review the requested accounts, network and signing action before approving.

### Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Features depend on the app version, network, wallet type and the service you connect to. Check the available actions in your installed app before transferring.
