Sui Network

SUI · Native network

In Oath · 5.0.2

How to receive SUI on iPhone

Open the wallet you want to receive into, tap Receive and choose Sui Network. On the Receive Sui Network screen, tap Copy address or let the sender scan your QR code. Ask them to select Sui Mainnet for the transfer.

When you receive
Showing your address or QR code does not submit a transaction. The outgoing transaction needs a gas payer. A sending exchange may also charge its own withdrawal fee.
When you send later
For an ordinary send from Oath, keep SUI in the same Sui account for gas and review the estimate before confirming.

The sender only sees another network

Stop at the network selection. Ask whether the sending service supports withdrawals on Sui Mainnet. Address appearance alone cannot confirm the network.

Receive Sui Network

  1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Sui Network in Receive.
  2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
  3. Share the Sui receiving address. For tokens, match the full coin type rather than relying on a name or icon.
  4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

Send SUI and pay network fees

  1. Open Send and select the asset on Sui Network. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
  2. SUI pays network fees. Review the transfer and selected asset; coin objects and gas requirements affect what can be sent.
  3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
  4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

Connect to dApps

  1. Open a trusted dApp and choose Oath if it is listed, or choose WalletConnect.
  2. On desktop, scan the connection QR with Oath. On iPhone or iPad, use a compatible app link or the connection option offered by the dApp.
  3. Review the website, selected wallet, Sui Network network and requested permissions before approving.
  4. Review each signing request separately. Disconnect the session from Oath when you are finished.

Questions

How do I receive Sui Network in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Sui Network in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Share the Sui receiving address. For tokens, match the full coin type rather than relying on a name or icon.

What pays the Sui Network transaction fee?

SUI is the native fee asset for this network account. SUI pays network fees. Review the transfer and selected asset; coin objects and gas requirements affect what can be sent.

Can I connect Sui Network dApps?

Connect to compatible Sui Network dApps through WalletConnect. The website must request a network and signing method supported by Oath. Review every connection and signing request in the app. A Sui Wallet Standard-only website is not automatically compatible with WalletConnect.

Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Keep recovery details private.

Oath’s website never needs your recovery phrase or private key. Share only the receiving address when someone needs to send you crypto.
