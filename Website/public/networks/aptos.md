# Aptos Wallet on iPhone – Find Your Address and Receive APT

Open Oath on your iPhone and select the wallet you want to fund. Tap Receive, search for APT and choose Aptos (APT). Tap Copy address or show the QR code. Ask the sender to send native APT to that address on Aptos Mainnet.

## Where is my Aptos wallet address?

1. In the Receive results, choose the native coin with the symbol APT. Other tokens can appear on Aptos too.
2. In the sending wallet or exchange, choose APT and the Aptos withdrawal network. Paste your copied address or scan the QR code, then compare the complete destination with the address on your iPhone before confirming.
3. After broadcast, look up the transaction hash in Aptos Explorer with Mainnet selected. Check the recipient, amount and status, then open the same wallet in Oath to see the deposit.

## Send APT

1. Open Send and select the asset on Aptos. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
2. Keep APT available for network fees when transferring supported assets.
3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

## dApp connections

Aptos send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.

## Receiving APT on iPhone

### Do I need an Aptos RPC URL to receive APT?

No. An RPC URL connects software to an Aptos node. The sender needs the public account address from your Receive screen. Aptos account addresses are 32 bytes and are commonly displayed as 0x followed by 64 hexadecimal characters. Copy the complete address as Oath shows it.

### Who pays the fee when someone sends me APT?

For an ordinary wallet transfer the sender pays the network fee in APT. An exchange may also charge a withdrawal fee. You can receive without an APT balance. Keep some APT for gas if you send funds later.

### My withdrawal is pending. How do I check it?

Look in the sending service’s withdrawal history for a transaction hash. If it has already broadcast the transfer, use that hash to check Aptos Mainnet. If it has not provided one, ask the sending service for the status before making another withdrawal.
