Litecoin

LTC · Bitcoin family

Receive Litecoin

  1. Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Litecoin in Receive.
  2. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender.
  3. Choose Litecoin in Receive and confirm that the sender is using Litecoin, not a wrapped LTC token on another chain.
  4. After the sender broadcasts the transfer, follow its status in Oath. Network confirmation can take time.

Send LTC and pay network fees

  1. Open Send and select the asset on Litecoin. Paste the recipient’s address or scan their QR.
  2. Litecoin transaction fees depend on the inputs and transaction size. Leave enough LTC for the fee or review the calculated Max.
  3. Enter the amount and any required memo or tag. Compare the complete destination with the recipient’s instructions.
  4. Review the asset, network, amount and fee, then confirm in Oath. Follow the transaction status after broadcast.

Connection availability

Litecoin send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.

Questions

How do I receive Litecoin in Oath?

Open Oath, select your wallet and choose Litecoin in Receive. Copy the receiving address or show its QR code. Confirm the exact network with the sender. Choose Litecoin in Receive and confirm that the sender is using Litecoin, not a wrapped LTC token on another chain.

What pays the Litecoin transaction fee?

LTC is the native fee asset for this network account. Litecoin transaction fees depend on the inputs and transaction size. Leave enough LTC for the fee or review the calculated Max.

Can I connect Litecoin dApps?

Litecoin send and receive support does not include a supported dApp connection route in this guide. Use Oath for transfers; do not import your recovery phrase into a website to connect.

Can I send to the same address on another network?

Only send using the network the recipient explicitly supports. A matching address or ticker does not bridge assets or make different chains interchangeable.

Keep recovery details private.

Oath’s website never needs your recovery phrase or private key. Share only the receiving address when someone needs to send you crypto.
